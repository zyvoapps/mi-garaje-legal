Privacy Policy

Mi Garaje by ZYVO · Last updated November 15, 2026 · Applies to Android version 0.4.21.

This policy explains how Mi Garaje by ZYVO, presented under the ZYVO technology brand, handles information. Privacy contact: zyvo.apps@gmail.com.

1. Data you enter

The app lets you record vehicle details such as make, model, year, license plate, mileage, and photos; fuel, maintenance, spare parts, issues, tire pressure, expenses, currencies, and exchange rates. You choose what to enter. License plates and photos may identify individuals.

2. Local storage

In the version covered by this policy, vehicle information and records are stored in the Android app's private local storage. No Mi Garaje cloud account or ZYVO-operated synchronization server currently receives these histories. Core functions can operate offline. Android may back up app data according to your device and account settings. Such system backups are controlled by the relevant operating-system service, not a Mi Garaje server.

3. Microphone and ZYVO voice assistant

Microphone permission is requested for voice interactions you deliberately start. The current version uses local Vosk speech recognition and available Android text-to-speech voices; Mi Garaje itself does not upload voice recordings to ZYVO servers. Listening stops when a conversation closes or is paused. Optional language-model ZIP files are selected and imported by the user.

4. Photos, files, and sharing

You can choose photos through Android's file picker and use them for vehicle records or local photo editing. JSON backup and restore files are saved or selected through Android's document picker. If you export a file or share a mechanic message, the information may leave Mi Garaje through your explicit action and may then be governed by the selected destination app or service.

5. Third-party exchange-rate requests

When you request a reference exchange rate, the app may connect over HTTPS to api.frankfurter.dev (Frankfurter), sending a requested date and currency code. No license plates, photos, vehicle records, or voice recordings are attached to that request. The external provider may process normal technical request information, including your IP address, under its own practices. These requests may fail offline.

6. Advertising, accounts, analytics, and purchases

Version 0.4.21 does not integrate live ads, active subscription payments, or sign-in accounts. The current free limit is two vehicles. The app describes a planned Premium tier but does not charge for it. Before introducing advertising, analytics, accounts, synchronization, or payments, this policy and any app-store data declarations will be updated to reflect the actual services and providers.

7. Purposes

Your records are used locally to display history, estimate costs and fuel efficiency, provide maintenance reminders, support searches, and prepare messages or backups. Mi Garaje does not sell vehicle histories to third parties.

8. Retention and deletion

App records remain on your device until you change or delete them or clear the app's data. Uninstalling normally removes local app storage, but exported files, system backups, or files shared with external services may remain. You are responsible for deleting backups and exports from their respective locations. There is currently no Mi Garaje account stored on proprietary servers that needs separate account deletion.

9. Security

The app uses Android's private app storage and HTTPS for exchange-rate requests. Protect your device, update its operating system, and securely store backups. No guarantee can be made for data on compromised devices or backups shared with others.

10. Children and third-party data

The app is intended as a vehicle-management tool and is not specifically designed for children. Avoid entering another person's personal information without proper authorization.

11. Updates and contact

This policy may change as the app evolves. The revision date will be shown here. For privacy questions or data requests, contact zyvo.apps@gmail.com.
